Who we are

This website belongs to The Lucky Locker, a thrift store at {{addressInline}}. The person responsible for it is {{privacyOwner}}. You can reach us through the contact page or by phone at {{phoneLink}}.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Email list: your email address, and your first name if you choose to give it.

That is all. The website has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels, and it sets no cookies of its own.

How we use it

  • To answer your message.
  • To send the email list to people who joined it, about new arrivals, seasonal stock and shop news.

We never sell or rent your details, and we don’t share them except with the service below that delivers the forms.

The services we use

  • Formspree receives what you type into our forms and passes it on to us. Formspree’s own privacy policy covers how it handles that data.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website. Like any web host it handles the technical details your browser sends, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and keep them secure.
  • Google Maps loads on the Visit page only if you press “Show the map”. Until then, nothing is sent to Google. The “Get directions” link opens Google Maps in a new tab.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept only as long as needed to reply. Email list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the email list at any time using the link in any email, or by asking us.
  • Ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you through the contact page.

Children

The website is not aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
