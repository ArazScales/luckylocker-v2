Frequently asked questions

Donations

Answer from Christine: yes or no, and whether you buy items or take consignment.

Answer from Christine: what you accept, and what you can’t take (for example cribs, car seats or large furniture).

Answer from Christine: drop-off days, hours and where to bring them.

Do I get a receipt for my donation?

Answer from Christine: whether you give donation receipts.

Shopping

What payment methods do you take?

Answer from Christine: cash, credit and debit cards, tap to pay, checks.

Can you hold an item for me?

Answer from Christine: whether you hold items, for how long, and if a deposit is needed.

Can I return or exchange something?

Answer from Christine: your returns policy, for example all sales final or exchanges within 7 days.

How often does new stock come out?

Answer from Christine: how often the shelves are restocked, and the best day to come.

Do you have sales?

Answer from Christine: regular sales such as color-tag days, and how people hear about them.

Do you sell online or ship?

Answer from Christine: in store only, or online too.
